# Lead Magnet Ideator: paste-in prompt (Founder Jam Starter Kit)

Works on any Claude plan, including Free. No upload needed.

**What you get:** 5-7 lead magnet ideas for your customer, scored against each other, with a short post you can use to test the top ideas before you build anything.

**Time:** 30-45 minutes.

**How to use:** run `icp-definer.md` first. Then open a new chat, copy everything inside the box below, paste your FOUNDER JAM HANDOFF block where it says to, and send.

---

```
Help me come up with and score lead magnet ideas: the free thing my ideal customer would trade their email for.

Here is my context from the ICP Definer:
[paste your FOUNDER JAM HANDOFF block here]

What I sell (my paid offer): [one sentence]

If anything you need is missing, ask me before you start, one question at a time.

1. Generate 5-7 concepts. Mix the three types (problem revealing, sample or trial, one step of a multi-step process) and the delivery methods (software, information, service, physical). Include at least one interactive tool (calculator, quiz, scorecard) and at least one guide or playbook. Give each a clear name built from who it's for, the result, a time frame, and the format. Clear beats clever.

For each concept:
- Type and delivery method
- Value in one sentence
- What it reveals or completes for the customer
- How it leads naturally to my paid offer
- Build estimate: EASY / MEDIUM / HARD, hours, tools

2. Score every concept 1-5 on: value (big outcome, feels achievable, fast, low effort), bridge to my paid offer, fit with my ideal customer, fit with what I already talk about, build speed (5 = under 4 hours, 1 = 40+), whether my channels can promote it, and reuse potential.
Composite = (value*3 + bridge*3 + fit*3 + congruence*2 + speed*2 + distribution*2 + reuse*1) / 16
Show a comparison table ranked by composite. Flag anything under 3.0 to rework or drop.

3. For the top 2-3: write a test post under 280 characters for where my customer spends time, and a 2-3 sentence version I could say out loud in a conversation.

4. Recommend the next step: 4.0+ means build it now; 3.0-3.9 means post the test first; under 3.0 means rethink the customer or the problem.

Rules: never invent statistics or customer stories; be honest about build time; if my customer is too broad to make specific ideas, make me narrow it first.
```
