# ICP Definer: paste-in prompt (Founder Jam Starter Kit)

Works on any Claude plan, including Free. No upload needed.

**What you get:** a specific first customer to go after, how your competitors are winning theirs, and the 1-2 channels to reach that customer in the next 30 days.

**Time:** about 60 minutes. Answer one question at a time; Claude will push back where your answers are vague.

**How to use:** open a new chat at claude.ai, copy everything inside the box below, fill in the brackets, and send.

---

```
Help me define my ideal first customer and how to reach them. Run three steps in order and wait for my answers as you go. Ask me one question at a time.

My startup:
- Name: [your startup name]
- Problem: [one sentence: what problem you solve]
- Who I think the customer is: [rough guess is fine]
- Stage: [idea / pre-revenue / early-revenue]
- Business type: [B2B SaaS / Services / Marketplace / Consumer]

Step 1: Ideal customer (Jobs-to-be-Done)
- Draft 2-3 distinct customer segments as "When [trigger], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome]"
- Score each 1-5 on pain intensity, willingness to pay, reachability, and my advantage
- Recommend one beachhead segment to own first (for a marketplace, define supply and demand separately and start with the harder side)
- Check the pick for red flags: "we can help anyone", enterprise and SMB at once, no trigger event, I can't name a real person who fits, demographics instead of problems
- Pressure-test it: who is the best fit and why, who we should not target, how they solve this today, what would make them say no, how they buy

Step 2: How competitors grow
- Identify 3-5 competitors, including the status quo (spreadsheets, an agency, doing nothing)
- For each: how they get customers (content, search, social, partnerships, paid, sales)
- Find the gaps: topics my customer cares about that nobody covers well
- One short battle card per competitor

Step 3: Where to show up
- Score 4-6 channels 1-5 on audience match, competition, time to traction, and my advantage
- Recommend 1-2 primary channels and one to ignore for now, with reasons
- Give me a 30-day plan to get my first 100 conversations without paid ads

Be direct. If my answer is generic, say so and ask again.

Finish with:
1. My ICP card (JTBD statement and profile)
2. The segment comparison table
3. The competitor battle cards
4. The channel scores and 30-day plan
5. This block, filled in, inside a code box so I can copy it into the next prompt:

FOUNDER JAM HANDOFF
startup_name:
problem_statement:
business_type:
stage:
icp_segment:
top_pains:
channels_selected:
```
