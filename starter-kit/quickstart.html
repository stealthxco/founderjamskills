What good looks like

Your ICP is specific when
  • you can name a real person who fits it
  • it has a trigger: something that just happened to them
  • it describes a problem, not a demographic
Your lead magnet is ready to test when
  • it scores 3.0 or higher
  • a working version takes days, not weeks
  • finishing it leads to what you sell
